# FFmpeg Video Editing Skill

## Key Automation Tasks
- **Quality Control**: Use `blackdetect`, `freezedetect`, and motion difference filters to prevent black frames or static image slideshows.
- **Audio Stems Mixing**: Balance voiceover (0 dB), sound effects (+1.5 to -3 dB relative), and music bed (-9.5 dB) with ducking and liming (`alimiter=limit=0.95`).
- **Mastering**: EBU R128 integrated loudness normalization to -14 LUFS with true peak <= -1.0 dBTP.
- **Subtitles Overlay**: Crisp font rendering with border stroke and translucent backing box for vertical 9:16 clarity.
