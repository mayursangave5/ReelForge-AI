# Motion Graphics & VFX Skill

## VFX Categories
- **Shockwaves**: Expanding concentric rings with decay, opacity fade, and SVG displacement.
- **Debris Particles**: Pseudo-random scattered fragments (`rnd`) with gravity acceleration and tumble.
- **Atmospheric Fire**: Layered gradient paths with turbulence filters and speed blur.
- **Speed Lines**: Dynamic radial and horizontal streaks during high-velocity camera sequences.
- **Infographic Diagrams**: Data counters, animated bezier trend lines, circular progress arcs, and schematic callout chips.
